06 / QUESTIONS PHYSICS DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY SETTLE

CAN THE UNIVERSE
FUNCTION WITHOUT GOD?

A mathematical system can evolve using internally stated rules without intervention. That is not evidence that God does not exist — and it does not establish a divine explanation. Science tests specific empirical claims; many general metaphysical claims lie outside the reach of these toys.

Physics models observed regularities and mechanisms under assumed mathematical rules. It does not, merely by computing a solution, determine why laws exist.
These are frameworks for interpretation, not measured probabilities or an automated test for God's existence.
