03 / MODEL ASSUMPTIONS

THE MATH
BEHIND THE MOTION.

The logistic recurrence is a textbook model of nonlinear dynamics. It is not a realistic forecast of population size without parameter estimation and validation. All values here are dimensionless.

ITERATIONxₙ₊₁ = r xₙ(1−xₙ)

A bounded nonlinear map for x in [0,1] and r in [0,4].

LOCAL STRETCHINGf′(x) = r(1−2x)

The derivative quantifies the local expansion or contraction of nearby trajectories.

FINITE-TIME EXPONENTλₙ = (1/n) Σ ln|f′(xᵢ)|

Positive values suggest sensitivity; near-zero values require longer runs and convergence checks.

VALIDATIONNUMERICAL ≠ EMPIRICAL

The diagrams visualize equations. They are not proof about the physical Universe.
