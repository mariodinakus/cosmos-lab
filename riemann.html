WHY IT MATTERS

THE LINK TO THE PRIMES

The explicit formula writes the prime-counting function as a main term plus a sum over zeros ρ of ζ. A zero off the line Re s = ½ would produce larger fluctuations in the primes than any known. The Hilbert–Pólya idea proposes that the zeros are eigenvalues of some self-adjoint operator; the GUE spacing seen here is the strongest numerical hint, but no such operator is known. The Riemann Hypothesis remains unproven.
