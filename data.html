REAL DATASTAT. ERRORS ONLY

Distance modulus of each supernova against redshift, fitted with a flat universe of matter and a dark-energy fluid of equation of state w. The absolute-magnitude offset (degenerate with H₀) is marginalised, so this fit measures the shape of expansion, not H₀.

μ(z) = 5 log₁₀[(1+z_hel) D_C(z_HD)] + offset E² = Ωm(1+z)³ + (1−Ωm)(1+z)^{3(1+w)} χ² = Σ (m_B − μ)² / σ²
Important limit. Only the diagonal statistical errors are used. The published analysis uses the full 1701×1701 statistical + systematic covariance matrix, so the uncertainties and contours here are too small. Reference: Pantheon+ alone gives flat ΛCDM Ωm = 0.334 ± 0.018 and flat wCDM w = −0.90 ± 0.14 (Brout et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 110). Calibrator supernovae are excluded and duplicates across surveys are treated as independent.

The data: apparent magnitude of each supernova (offset removed) against redshift, with the best-fit ΛCDM curve. Fainter at fixed z than a decelerating universe predicts is the signature of acceleration.

Left: binned residuals relative to the best ΛCDM fit, with Einstein–de Sitter (Ωm = 1) and the best wCDM model; each model’s offset refitted. Right: Δχ² over (Ωm, w); cyan bands are 68.3%, 95.4% and 99.7% for two parameters.

Profile χ²(Ωm) for flat ΛCDM. The dashed line is the published Pantheon+ value, which has a wider error bar because it includes systematics.

DATA PROVENANCE

WHERE THE NUMBERS COME FROM

Redshifts, corrected magnitudes and diagonal errors come from the public Pantheon+SH0ES data release (github.com/PantheonPlusSH0ES/DataRelease), 1701 light curves of 1550 distinct supernovae. Cite: Brout et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 110 (arXiv:2202.04077); Scolnic et al. 2022, ApJ 938, 113. BAO and H(z) chronometer datasets are not included here because no verified machine-readable copy was available to embed; they can be uploaded in the Research Observatory.
