THE CRITICAL QUESTION

WHAT WOULD
ACTUALLY COUNT AS
DISCOVERY?

A scientific theory must explain existing observations, make testable predictions and survive attempts to falsify them. These experiments show the internal behavior of deliberately chosen equations — not a new theory of gravity.

01 / EXACT IN THE TOY

Entropy of independent pure entangled pairs, shortest paths in a graph and causal relations inside a prescribed spacetime are mathematically well defined.

02 / HEURISTIC, NOT DERIVED

Calling graph distances “geometry” or a rematched pair entropy a “Page curve” is a useful analogy, not a derivation of Einstein equations or black-hole evaporation.

03 / THE RESEARCH FRONTIER

Holographic entanglement, causal sets, black-hole information and microscopic spacetime remain active research areas. This laboratory keeps their assumptions visible.
